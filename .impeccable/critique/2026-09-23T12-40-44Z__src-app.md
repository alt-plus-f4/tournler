## Design Specificity Verdict
Mostly category-generic. "The Broadcast Booth" shows up only on the match page (face-off score, veto pips, connect strip). Home, teams, information and tournament overview read like a shadcn template. The hosted-server positioning never appears in the UI. The signal-only color rule is broken throughout. Roboto is loaded at 400 only, so every heavy name is faux bold. Detector: CLI 0 findings on src/app + src/components (3 false positives in the email template when scanning src). Overlay: low contrast (#737373 on black at 4.2–4.4:1, white on red-500 at 3.8:1), 10px meaningful labels, a skipped heading (h1→h5 on /teams), image hover transforms, nested cards on the match page, and a grid-stripe background flagged on every page.
